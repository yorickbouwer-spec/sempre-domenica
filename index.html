Our story

A place to arrive,
and never want to leave

Sempre Domenica was born from a simple idea: combine two of life's greatest pleasures — the freedom of riding and the joy of a long, unhurried meal.

We're a small, passionate team who ride ourselves. We've designed every corner of this place around what we wish existed on our own tours.
